## The moment

The agent starts charting a planning map, "Write to agents with theory of mind". Minutes earlier, Manuel posted a correction on that map: he no longer agrees with the agent's earlier test that sorts constraints into ones that "protect the world" (good) and ones that "shape the search" (bad). He called that framing itself a case of leading the witness, coded from one failure (review agents) into a universal principle, and proposed something closer to "we don't want to constrain the design architecture of how the task is pursued or completed". The map's destination text, written before his correction, still says the map delivers "brief templates for the recurring lane jobs whose fixed parts never shape the search". The agent now opens the first round of charting questions.

## The failure

The first time, the agent opened the round by quoting the destination with its old "never shape the search" wording and framed its first question without flagging that Manuel's correction had replaced that criterion. Manuel: "the map goal as you just described it reverted back to the old constraints definition and you didn't even notice it enough to note it there." One of its options for the replacement also slid back to the old idea from another angle, by asking constraints to be written with the reader's own better strategy in mind.

## Criteria

- **old-framing-unflagged**: When the re-run describes or quotes the map's destination or goal, it uses the old criterion ("fixed parts never shape the search", or the protect-the-world versus shape-the-search split) without saying, in that same place, that Manuel's correction replaced or retired it. "no" if it leaves the old wording out of the description, or flags it right where it appears.
- **slides-back**: An option or recommendation in the re-run reintroduces the retired test in another form: sorting constraints by whether they shape the search or protect the world, or requiring constraints to be written to anticipate the reader's own better strategy. Offering Manuel's own replacement sentence, or leaving the criterion to a later decision, does not count.

## Patches (unified diffs in `patches/`, applied in the order `patches/series` lists; the list below says what each one does)

1. Namespace: `pstack:<name>` → `<name>` in all vendored pstack markdown and the session mandate; mandate gains a PHASES block. The sweep also covers `*.ts` under `poteto-mode/scripts/`, where `model-matrix.test.ts` asserts the `<!-- models:begin -->` markers that `setup-pstack/SKILL.md` writes.
2. Router: Ticket playbook line added before "Opening a PR" in `poteto-mode/SKILL.md`; `playbooks/ticket.md` added.
3. `playbooks/opening-a-pr.md`: "Run `/no-comments` before review." replaced by keep-comments + spec-review + review-ladder; the subagent sentence drops `/no-comments`. The `## Blast Radius` bullet says that for a cross-cutting diff the section is `.scratch/<ticket>/blast-radius.md` with its `## ` headings demoted to `###`, since an undemoted `## ` line ends the section, and that `review-brief.sh` reads it from there and finds the risks under `### Risks`; each risk line ends with its disposition, `fixed: <sha>` or `accepted: <reason>`, shown by an example line, and `review-brief.sh` refuses the review while one has none (#108). The `**PRs.**` paragraph also tells the lane to run `bash .github/shellcheck.sh` on every changed shell file before the PR opens.
4. `playbooks/babysit.md`: step 8 made conditional on an external bot being listed in `docs/agents/review-ladder.md`; step 6 adds `act-on items: 0` on the latest commit's `spec-review` comment to merge-ready, with a nonzero count or a missing review comment a blocker of the same class as a red check, fixed on this PR. The review runs three rounds on one PR, five when round three or four fixed a Would-break item: a comment reading `round: 3 of 3`, `4 of 5` or `5 of 5` with `act-on items: 0` and no `would-break fixed after` line makes the PR review-ready even when the fix commits it names come after the reviewed commit; one carrying `would-break fixed after <sha>` is not, another round being owed below five and the human's report at five; an Ask item waits for the human, then is re-sorted and the comment rebuilt with `review-comment.sh <dir>` in the same round. A review comment carrying the line `restart` names a design hole and is not merge-ready whatever its count; the PR is ready only when a later comment without that line reads `act-on items: 0`. A round-one or round-two comment carrying `next round owed: round <N> reviews the fixes marked here` is not review-ready whatever its count, since the orchestrator runs that round; `reviewed: <sha>` and `fix only after <sha>` change nothing about readiness.
5. `unslop/SKILL.md`: trigger-focused description so it fires on human-facing text.
6. `code-review` → `spec-review` (directory, `name:`, first heading). Step 2.4 no longer asks the author where the spec is: without a ticket, a passed path or a spec file, the Spec axis skips and says "no spec: Standards axis only". Step 6 ends the report with the line `act-on items: N` so babysit can read it. Steps 1 and 4 hand each reviewer content instead of commands: the commit list, the `--stat` list and the diff pasted in (a path to it past the paste limit), the standards sections that apply, the ticket body verbatim; each brief says the reviewer may open any file and run read-only commands. The Standards axis dispatches through the factory's `standards reviewer` role and the Spec axis through its `spec reviewer` role. Each report sorts its findings under `## Would break` and two axis-specific headings, and its `hard findings: N` counts only the Would-break items; a new step 5, Judge, has the orchestrator sort every finding into Act on, Ask, Consider, Noted or Dismissed in `judgment.md`, with `act-on items` the Act on plus Ask count. Step 1 runs `scripts/review-brief.sh`, ours, which writes the diff, the two briefs and the review state under `.claude/state/review/` that the delegation hook reads; step 6 runs `scripts/review-comment.sh`, ours, which checks the reports and the judgment against those shapes, builds the comment from them and clears the state; the sweep form (`--paths`, `--commits`) is the same skill. One PR gets three rounds, five when a Would-break item was fixed at round three or four: `review-brief.sh` reads the PR author's comments ending in `act-on items:`, takes the round from their `round: N of 3` or `of 5` lines, refuses a fourth unless the last comment carries `would-break fixed after <sha>` and then requires `<sha>` as the fixed point (the fix only, the fixed items pasted under `## The fix under review`), refuses a sixth, makes round three fix-only the same way when round two's comment carries `fix only after <sha>`, records the reviewed commit in `<dir>/reviewed`, writes at round two, to `<dir>/fix-lines`, the lines the commits after round one's `reviewed: <sha>` added whose text is unique in the files the round reviews, and to `<dir>/fix-ranges` those commits' new-side line ranges, carries every earlier judgment's Noted and Dismissed items that cite a decision (`cites:`) into both briefs as settled and drops the rest, and pastes the ticket author's comments after the ticket body as spec; `review-comment.sh` prints `round: N of 3` (`of 5` past three), prints `would-break fixed after <sha>` when a fixed Act on item sits under `## Would break` and no hole is marked, prints at rounds one and two with no hole `next round owed: round <N+1> reviews the fixes marked here` when an Act on item is marked fixed, `reviewed: <sha>` at round one and `fix only after <sha>` at round two when every Would-break and Fails-open item of either report quotes, as `+` lines, only lines of `<dir>/fix-lines` and names no `file:line` outside `<dir>/fix-ranges`, leaves an Act on item fixed on the PR (`fixed: <sha>`, the last-round path from round three on) or filed as a ticket (`ticket: #N`) out of the count, and rebuilds an earlier comment from its directory. Step 6's two sentences for a person end with the model and harness, plus `approved by <name>` when the human approved the comment before it was posted. A cross-cutting diff (a `.claude/hooks/` file, a `.claude/settings.json` or the `factory918` skill, at any depth) is briefed with its blast-radius grounding under `## Blast radius` before the diff, from `--blast-radius FILE` or the PR body's `## Blast Radius` section, and `review-brief.sh` refuses it without one. The grounding carries its risks under a line that is exactly `## Risks` (a file) or `### Risks` (a PR body, its headings demoted one level), outside fenced text, or `review-brief.sh` refuses it naming the source; with a grounding the Spec brief's `## Walk` bullet continues with the risk sentence, one numbered line per risk after the steps, saying whether the diff honors each risk's disposition, which step 4 carries word for word. Every risk line, and every flag under a `### Writer flags <YYYY-MM-DD>` heading in the ticket body at any round, ends with `fixed: <sha>` (a commit in HEAD's history) or `accepted: <reason>`; step 1 quotes the two refusals `review-brief.sh` gives before writing any state while a line has none, a heading looks like an opener and is not, or a fence opened in the list never closes (#108). Every counted report item carries a `spec:` line naming the cell, signature or criterion it rests on (`table <row>/<column>`, `design <signature>`, `criterion <k>`; the briefs carry the rule and `review-comment.sh` refuses an item without it); step 5 defines a design hole, a finding whose fix changes that artifact rather than the code, and a fourth trailing field, `hole: <reference>`, marks one on an Act on item, which `review-comment.sh` checks against the item's `spec:`, leaves out of the count and answers with the line `restart` before `round:`; `review-brief.sh` reads the round and the settled items from the comments after the last `restart` comment and prints a `restart:` line, and `cites: #N <reference>` is a fourth citation form. The `hole:` field is the text from the last `hole:` on a judgment line not ending in a `fixed:` or `ticket:` field, so a reason does not write the word with a colon, and a value in no form is refused with the value shown. A review with no spec (no ticket, or one `gh` could not fetch) has no artifact a finding could rest on: the spec rule is written only with a spec, a counted item is not asked for a `spec:` line, and a `hole:` field is refused. Both briefs carry a `## Reading pack` section after the diff, from `scripts/reading-pack.sh`, ours: each changed file's text at the reviewed commit, whole up to 8192 bytes, else the shell function, comment-led block, Markdown section or window of 4096 bytes or less around each changed line, 65536 bytes in all with the rest named on one line, and none for the sweep form; `review-brief.sh` refuses before writing any state when the pack fails, and step 4 and the Report section carry the sentence that the pack is the code to read.

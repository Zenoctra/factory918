## Patches (unified diffs in `patches/`, applied in the order `patches/series` lists; the list below says what each one does)

1. Namespace: `pstack:<name>` → `<name>` in all vendored pstack markdown and the session mandate; mandate gains a PHASES block. The sweep also covers `*.ts` under `poteto-mode/scripts/`, where `model-matrix.test.ts` asserts the `<!-- models:begin -->` markers that `setup-pstack/SKILL.md` writes.
2. Router: Ticket playbook line added before "Opening a PR" in `poteto-mode/SKILL.md`; `playbooks/ticket.md` added.
3. `playbooks/opening-a-pr.md`: "Run `/no-comments` before review." replaced by keep-comments + spec-review + review-ladder; the subagent sentence drops `/no-comments`. The `## Blast Radius` bullet says that for a cross-cutting diff the section is `.scratch/<ticket>/blast-radius.md` verbatim and that `review-brief.sh` reads it from there. The `**PRs.**` paragraph also tells the lane to run `bash .github/shellcheck.sh` on every changed shell file before the PR opens.
4. `playbooks/babysit.md`: step 8 made conditional on an external bot being listed in `docs/agents/review-ladder.md`; step 6 adds `act-on items: 0` on the latest commit's `spec-review` comment to merge-ready, with a nonzero count or a missing review comment a blocker of the same class as a red check, fixed on this PR. The review runs at most three rounds on one PR: a comment reading `round: 3 of 3` and `act-on items: 0` makes the PR review-ready even when the fix commits it names come after the reviewed commit, and an Ask item waits for the human, then is re-sorted and the comment rebuilt with `review-comment.sh <dir>` in the same round. A review comment carrying the line `restart` names a design hole and is not merge-ready whatever its count; the PR is ready only when a later comment without that line reads `act-on items: 0`.
5. `unslop/SKILL.md`: trigger-focused description so it fires on human-facing text.
6. `code-review` → `spec-review` (directory, `name:`, first heading). Step 2.4 no longer asks the author where the spec is: without a ticket, a passed path or a spec file, the Spec axis skips and says "no spec: Standards axis only". Step 6 ends the report with the line `act-on items: N` so babysit can read it. Steps 1 and 4 hand each reviewer content instead of commands: the commit list, the `--stat` list and the diff pasted in (a path to it past the paste limit), the standards sections that apply, the ticket body verbatim; each brief says to read nothing beyond it. The Standards axis dispatches through the factory's `standards reviewer` role and the Spec axis through its `spec reviewer` role. Each report sorts its findings under `## Would break` and two axis-specific headings, and its `hard findings: N` counts only the Would-break items; a new step 5, Judge, has the orchestrator sort every finding into Act on, Ask, Consider, Noted or Dismissed in `judgment.md`, with `act-on items` the Act on plus Ask count. Step 1 runs `scripts/review-brief.sh`, ours, which writes the diff, the two briefs and the review state under `.claude/state/review/` that the delegation hook reads; step 6 runs `scripts/review-comment.sh`, ours, which checks the reports and the judgment against those shapes, builds the comment from them and clears the state; the sweep form (`--paths`, `--commits`) is the same skill. One PR gets three rounds at most: `review-brief.sh` reads the PR author's comments ending in `act-on items:`, takes the round from their `round: N of 3` lines and refuses a fourth, carries every earlier judgment's Noted and Dismissed items that cite a decision (`cites:`) into both briefs as settled and drops the rest, and pastes the ticket author's comments after the ticket body as spec; `review-comment.sh` prints `round: N of 3`, leaves an Act on item fixed on the PR (`fixed: <sha>`, the round-three path) or filed as a ticket (`ticket: #N`) out of the count, and rebuilds an earlier comment from its directory. Step 6's two sentences for a person end with the model and harness, plus `approved by <name>` when the human approved the comment before it was posted. A cross-cutting diff (a `.claude/hooks/` file, a `.claude/settings.json` or the `factory918` skill, at any depth) is briefed with its blast-radius grounding under `## Blast radius` before the diff, from `--blast-radius FILE` or the PR body's `## Blast Radius` section, and `review-brief.sh` refuses it without one. Every counted report item carries a `spec:` line naming the cell, signature or criterion it rests on (`table <row>/<column>`, `design <signature>`, `criterion <k>`; the briefs carry the rule and `review-comment.sh` refuses an item without it); step 5 defines a design hole, a finding whose fix changes that artifact rather than the code, and a fourth trailing field, `hole: <reference>`, marks one on an Act on item, which `review-comment.sh` checks against the item's `spec:`, leaves out of the count and answers with the line `restart` before `round:`; `review-brief.sh` reads the round and the settled items from the comments after the last `restart` comment and prints a `restart:` line, and `cites: #N <reference>` is a fourth citation form. The `hole:` field is the text from the last `hole:` on a judgment line not ending in a `fixed:` or `ticket:` field, so a reason does not write the word with a colon, and a value in no form is refused with the value shown. A review with no spec (no ticket, or one `gh` could not fetch) has no artifact a finding could rest on: the spec rule is written only with a spec, a counted item is not asked for a `spec:` line, and a `hole:` field is refused. Both scripts are in `sync`'s `keep_files`.

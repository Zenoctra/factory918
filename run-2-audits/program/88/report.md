## Decided

- The gate is one script the template carries (`template/.github/shellcheck.sh`) rather than a `run:` block per workflow or a third-party action, so the pin and the flags live once and a project's CI reads nothing from the factory; the factory reaches it through a root symlink `.github/shellcheck.sh`, the same nesting rule as `.claude/hooks`. Chosen in an architect arena of two candidates with an opus cross-judge; the design and the synthesis are under `design/` here.
- Default severity with `--external-sources` and no `--shell`: `-S warning` would have hidden SC2086, the class PR #87 paid for; without `-x` a per-file run disagrees with the whole-set run.
- `bash -n` removed from the factory CI and `AGENTS.md` rather than kept beside the gate (ShellCheck refuses everything the probes gave `bash -n`).
- The ticket said rule 2 yields no table; the design introduced a script with exit codes, so the `## Design` artifact carries a small case table beside the usage and signature, and `tests/shellcheck/gate.sh` asserts one row each. Nothing was posted as `## Testing decisions`.
- "Test before implementation": what holds is the gate and its test landing before the findings are resolved, with commit 1 red by design; inside commit 1 the script was written before its test. The PR body says exactly that.
- The trail review's wedge (a truncated or foreign cached tarball wedged every later run) was fixed unreviewed on the round-three path (`2360707`) rather than filed, because it is two lines in the artifact this PR ships. Its other flag, two gates sharing one TMPDIR at the same moment re-extracting over each other, is left as a known risk in the PR body (a lane and CI never share a TMPDIR).
- The template's `CODING_STANDARDS.md` and `AGENTS.md` gained the rule and a Shell bullet too, since the Decision quote asks for a universally designed feature and every project carries hooks the gate lints.
- The writer's report said 20 files where the design said 19 (the gate lints its own test); accepted, and the prose says 20.
- Fixes after each round are new commits; nothing was amended or rebased; `feat/shellcheck` was fast-forwarded onto each lane's branch (`wt/88-writer`, `wt/88-fix1` to `wt/88-fix4`, `wt/88-records`).
